# Combined GTP programme: design proposal

Prepared 6 October 2026 for team layout approval. No production page or CMS content has been changed.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or share that single file. All fonts and images are embedded, so it also works offline. For a quick team review, share `desktop.jpg` and `mobile.jpg`.

The proposal groups the combined page by date. Each day has one Register here button, Action Workshop poster cards, then Research Sessions tables grouped by hall. This makes the simultaneous timing visible while keeping the workshop cards requested by the team.

Date links, workshop scrolling and artwork previews work. Register here opens a design preview of the agreed email-eligibility step and shared form flow. The mockup does not accept an email, contact an API or submit a registration.

Research names, titles, venues and row counts are placeholders; research timing is pending final confirmation. Current workshop posters/titles show only a subset of the existing programme as layout references. The research introduction is draft copy. Approving the layout does not confirm these content details.

The main programme's multiple-host roundtable popup is planned separately in [the feedback plan](../../gtp-team-feedback-plan-2026-10-06.md); it is outside this subpage mockup.

Current-site images and fonts are credited in `asset-sources.json`. No AI-generated imagery was used. The practical authoring source is the self-contained HTML, and the screenshots record the proposed desktop/mobile layout.

Review completed at 1280px desktop and 390px mobile. Both captures have no page-wide horizontal overflow. Workshop fallback cards keep the same height as poster cards; research timing is explicitly pending. Date links, carousel controls, artwork previews and registration-preview close/focus behavior were checked. The final visual review found no remaining material fixes. This validates the proposal, not the production registration flow.
