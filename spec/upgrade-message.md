As we build the MPN portal, we need to upgrade two services that power the website.

**Supabase** is our database — it stores all member profiles, resources, publications, and Café activity. The free plan pauses automatically after a week of low activity and has no backups, which isn't reliable for a live community. The paid plan ($25/month) keeps it always-on with daily backups.

**Vercel** hosts our website. The free plan is intended for personal projects only — since SCPH is an organisation, we need the paid plan to stay within their terms of use. It also gives us more bandwidth and better performance as the portal grows ($20/month).

**Total: $45/month** to run the MPN portal reliably and safely.
